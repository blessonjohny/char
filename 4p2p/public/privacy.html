Privacy & Terms

28gulan.com · Last updated: 2 October 2026

In short: you can play as a guest — there are no accounts. We keep very little about you, we don't record voice chat, we don't sell your data, and we are not responsible for what other players say in chat or on voice. The site is meant for people aged 13 and over.

1. Who this is for

The card and board games on this site are free for everyone to look at and to play against the computer bots. The social features — table chat, voice chat and public comments — are intended for people aged 13 and older. If you are under 13, please play against the bots and do not use chat, voice or comments, and do not give out personal details.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has shared personal information with us, please tell us (see “Contact”) and we will remove it.

2. What is stored on your own device

To make the games work, your browser stores things like your player name, chosen avatar, game settings, saved games and the ID that lets you rejoin a table after a refresh (browser storage / cookies). This stays on your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

3. What our server handles

  • Your player name and avatar — shown to the other players at your table. Names containing abusive words are not accepted.
  • Game results and leaderboards — names and results of games may appear on leaderboards.
  • Table chat — messages are visible to the players at that table. Recent messages are kept with the table for a limited time so someone who reconnects can catch up. Abusive words are masked automatically, but the filter is not perfect.
  • Public comments — if you choose to leave a comment, it is stored so we can read and answer it. Please don't put personal details in a comment.
  • Visitor log (fair play) — to spot cheating, we keep an admin-only log of visits with an approximate location derived from your IP address, for up to 90 days. It is backed up so it survives server restarts. It is not used for advertising.
  • Accounts — there is no sign-up or login at the moment; guest play is all there is. If sign-in is added later, this page will be updated first.

We do not sell your information.

4. Voice chat

  • Voice goes directly between the players on a call (peer-to-peer). We do not record it and we do not store it.
  • To connect the call, browsers use helper (relay) servers that may briefly handle connection data, such as IP addresses, while the call is running. We do not keep voice audio.
  • Everyone on the call can hear you. We are not responsible for what other players say or do, on voice or in chat. Don't share personal details such as your real name, phone number or address.
  • You can leave voice at any time by tapping the mic button again.

5. Terms of use

  • The games are free and use virtual points and chips only — no real money is played for or won.
  • Be respectful. Abuse, harassment, hate speech, cheating or sharing others' personal information may get you removed from a table or blocked.
  • Chat, voice, names and comments are created by players, not by us. We don't check them in advance and are not responsible for them, but we may remove content or players.
  • The site is provided “as is”, without promises that it will always be available or error-free. Play at your own risk. We may change or stop parts of the site at any time.
  • If we change these terms or the privacy notice, the new version appears on this page with a new date.

6. Your choices

  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Play offline against the bots to avoid all interaction with other people.
  • Ask us to remove a comment or other information you gave us (see “Contact”).

7. Contact

Use the “Leave a Comment” option on the home page of 28gulan.com to reach us, including for privacy questions or removal requests.
