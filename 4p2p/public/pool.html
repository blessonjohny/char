How to Play

Objective

Pocket all your assigned balls (solids 1-7 or stripes 9-15), then legally pocket the 8-ball to win!

Controls

Phase 1 - AIM: Touch the TABLE to aim from the guide-line side (stick tip points to your finger). Touch anywhere on the STICK to aim from the butt side (stick follows your finger from behind). Move your finger to fine-tune. Release to lock aim.

Phase 2 - POWER: Touch anywhere and pull back. The further you pull, the harder you hit. Release to shoot!

Reset: Hold your finger still for 1 second to reset aim.
Spin: Tap the Spin button, then drag the cue ball icon for english (top, back, sides).
Zoom: Pinch with two fingers or use the + / - buttons.

Rules

First ball pocketed determines your group. Cue ball scratch = opponent gets ball-in-hand. Must pocket the 8-ball last to win. Foul = turn passes to opponent.
