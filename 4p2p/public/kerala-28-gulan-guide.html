How to Play

The Rules — 4-Player and 6-Player

28 is played two ways in Kerala: the traditional 4-player game, and a 6-player variant with a slightly bigger deck. The core idea — bid, hide a trump, win tricks — is the same in both. The deal and the bidding are where they differ.

1 The Deck

32 cards for four players, 36 for six

The 4-player game uses 32 cards — ranks 7 through Ace in all four suits. The 6-player game adds a 6 of each suit on top of that, for 36 cards total, so the deck divides evenly six ways.

2 The Deal & First Bid

Two-stage deal for 4 players, one deal for 6

4 players: everyone is dealt 4 cards first, and a first round of bidding happens right away, before anyone sees their full hand. Bids run 14 to 28.

6 players: all 6 cards go out to every player at once, then a single round of bidding follows — no split deal.

3 The Trump

One card hidden, face-down

The highest bidder names a trump suit and sets aside one trump card, face-down, unseen by anyone else. That hidden card can be called into play once, later in the hand — the well-timed reveal is where 28 gets tactical. This works the same way in both the 4-player and 6-player game.

4 4-Player Only: Second Bid

Four more cards, then a second round of bidding

In the 4-player game specifically, everyone is then dealt 4 more cards — 8 total — and a second round of bidding follows before play begins. The 6-player game skips this: with all 6 cards already dealt up front, there's only ever the one bidding round.

5 The Play

Trick by trick

Play proceeds trick by trick — follow suit if you can, and the Jack of trump, the Gulan, beats every other card in the game, including the Nine and Ace of the same suit. A 4-player hand runs 8 tricks (8 cards each); a 6-player hand runs 6 tricks (6 cards each).

6 The Score

Make your bid, or lose the hand

The bidding team needs to win at least as many points as they bid. Fail, and the round goes to the other side.

Questions Players Actually Ask

Frequently Asked

What is the 28 card game?

28 is a trick-taking card game from Kerala, India, played with 32 cards across 4 suits. Teams of two (or three, in the 6-player version) bid for the right to choose trump, and the Jack — the Gulan — is the highest card in every suit.

Why is 28 also called Gulan or Thuruppu Gulan?

The Jack of trump is the single most powerful card in the game, and it's known as the Gulan. "Thuruppu" means trump in Malayalam — so "Thuruppu Gulan" literally means "the trump Jack," and the name became shorthand for the whole game.

Is 28 the same as Twenty Eight, Kerala 28, Erupathettu or Irupathiyettu?

Yes — all the same game. 28, Twenty Eight, Kerala 28, Erupathettu, Thuruppu Gulan and Irupathiyettu (the Malayalam word for "twenty-eight") are different names for one card game, used across different regions and generations of players.

How many players do you need?

The traditional game is 4-handed, two teams of two, with a 32-card deck (7 through Ace) dealt in two stages — 8 cards each, two rounds of bidding. A popular variant, often called Kerala 6, is played 6-handed, two teams of three, with a 36-card deck (adding a 6 of each suit) dealt all at once — 6 cards each, one round of bidding.
