How to Play

The Rules, in Four Moves

1 — The Deal

Four cards each, then a bid

Four players sit as two teams, partners facing each other. Everyone gets four cards face-down. Based on just those four, players bid — starting from 14, going up to 28 — for the right to name trump. Whoever bids highest wins the auction.

2 — The Trump

One card hidden, face-down

The winning bidder names a trump suit and sets aside one trump card, face-down, unseen by anyone else. That hidden card can be called into play once, later in the hand — the well-timed reveal is where 28 gets tactical.

3 — The Play

Four more cards, then tricks

Each player is dealt four more cards, for eight total. Play proceeds trick by trick — follow suit if you can, and the Jack of trump, the Gulan, beats every other card in the game, including the Nine and Ace of the same suit.

4 — The Score

Make your bid, or lose the hand

The bidding team needs to win at least as many points as they bid. Fail, and the round goes to the other side.

Questions Players Actually Ask

Frequently Asked

What is the 28 card game?

28 is a trick-taking card game from Kerala, India, played with 32 cards across 4 suits. Teams of two (or three, in the 6-player version) bid for the right to choose trump, and the Jack — the Gulan — is the highest card in every suit.

Why is 28 also called Gulan or Thuruppu Gulan?

The Jack of trump is the single most powerful card in the game, and it's known as the Gulan. "Thuruppu" means trump in Malayalam — so "Thuruppu Gulan" literally means "the trump Jack," and the name became shorthand for the whole game.

Is 28 the same as Twenty Eight, Kerala 28, Eripathettu or Irupathiyettu?

Yes — all the same game. 28, Twenty Eight, Kerala 28, Eripathettu, Thuruppu Gulan and Irupathiyettu (the Malayalam word for "twenty-eight") are different names for one card game, used across different regions and generations of players.

How many players do you need?

The traditional game is 4-handed, two teams of two sitting across from each other. A popular variant — often called Kerala 6 — is played 6-handed, two teams of three.
